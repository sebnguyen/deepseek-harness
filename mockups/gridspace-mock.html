walk me through your idea for the grid space effect

The concept

The dot grid becomes a stack of planes at staggered depths, all centered on the same point. While we infer, the camera eases forward — planes bloom toward you, each fading in from the deep and dissolving as it passes the lens, so the tunnel feels endless.

Toggle start inference and watch the layer travel. Idle drifts gently backward-of-forward; inferencing is the sprint.

Why it could work

The UI stays readable because far planes fade before they dominate the type, and the sheer travel reads as thought without anything physically moving the page.
