Design notes — v2 changes

  • DO color template. Canonical DigitalOcean blue #0069FF drives the accent, tag, links and chips; the signature strip is now the ocean ramp #0052E6 → #0069FF → #00B3F0 → #00E5C8 instead of Mistral's warm-to-cool spectrum.
  • Dark mode as the hero. The sketch now opens on midnight navy surfaces (#0B1626 panel on #060C1A page) with ambient blue glows; the toggle flips to the azure light variant.
  • A floating element. The dialog rides a three-layer cast shadow plus a soft blue ambience, and idles on a 7s drift loop — it reads as hovering over the session, not docked to it.
  • Click a card, it fills the frame. A FLIP morph: the stage scales from the clicked cell's exact rect up to the full dialog (520ms, cubic-bezier(.22,.9,.28,1)), grid untouched behind it, and folds back to the same cell on close.
  • Video + explainer. The expanded stage splits 44/56: a media half (hue-shifted gradient per capability, play button, running progress bar, DEMO · 0:42 chip) and an explainer half with crumb, 30px title, description, keyword chips, and Prev/Next so all nine are browsable without returning to the grid.
  • Light shafts in every video. Each capability film streams sunlight down into its sea: 22 seeded god-ray beams (varying width, blur, drift, and opacity, blended screen over the pane's --h hue) swaying on slow sine-a-likes, under a breathing surface glow at the top edge. Deterministic seed keeps screenshots and reloads identical.
