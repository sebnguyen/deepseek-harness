Conversation redesign — chat that reads as turns, not rows

Static mockup over the live token sheet. Targets ui-chat/ChatView, ui-tool/ToolRow (24px rows), ui-trajectory/TrajectoryTurnHeader (44px sticky bar), ui-sidebar/SidebarRoot. Companion to chat-span-fold-mockup.html and composer-redesign-mockup.html; folds and composer are not re-litigated here.

1. A turn is one unit: the 44px sticky bar becomes a 24px divider; metrics ride the divider, and one condensed chip stays sticky.

2. Process is batched: same-weight tool rows collapse into semantic groups on a shared rail; only failures, questions, and approvals stand.

3. The answer stands apart from the process that produced it.

4. The sidebar reports state — running, waiting, failed, context pressure — not just titles.

1 · Current — a turn is a wall of equal rows

Replica of the shipped geometry: sticky 44px bar with four 71px metric columns per turn, then full-width bubble and a flat run of 24px rows — every read, every command, every delegation at the same visual weight, each with its own chevron. The running row sweeps. Scanning for “what happened” means reading every line.

3 · The group is the new row rule

A group answers “what kind of work, what outcome”: verb + object + count, status dot, and a right-aligned metric in the code face. Success collapses by default; the sweep moves from every running row to the one group head that is live. The per-call purpose line — currently shown on every frame — demotes to a hover second voice on the sub-row. Failed calls and open questions never fold: they float.

4 · Turn chrome: divider at rest, chip on scroll rule

At rest the turn opens with a hairline divider: TURN n plus the four metrics the 44px bar used to own, in the caption tier — 24px of chrome instead of 44, and it scrolls away. While a later turn is on screen, one condensed chip (top right, elevation-soft) names the live turn; clicking it is the existing TurnNavigator jump. Sticky space is spent once, not once per turn.
