The harness feels like two products glued by a toggle — blue in light, teal in dark, tokens from four other design systems. Can we make it one?
Thought for  4.2s · read theme sheets, token census

Yes — land one accent family and namespace the sheet. Concretely:

  • Accent: keep --ds-accent-fill blue in both themes; dark gets #2691EA fills and #61C8F9 text instead of teal.
  • Names: collapse --dsw-*/--dsh-*/--ds-* into one --ds-* layer set (surface · ink · line · fill · state · syntax).
  • States: derive tints with color-mix from walrus rungs — the Tailwind literals in state-*-bg disappear.

The semantic layer becomes the only place themes differ — full sheet in §4:
